## ✝️ Christianity & the Shadow

Traditional Christianity (especially Western, post-Augustinian) frames the shadow as:

> **“The sinful nature.”**  
Something to **fight, deny, confess, cleanse, crucify**.

There’s power in that metaphor —  
but **no relationship** is ever formed.

You’re taught to:

- Fear the wolf  
- Shame the wolf  
- Nail it to a cross

But you’re **never taught to ask the wolf what it wants.**

---

## 🧘‍♂️ Jung’s Alternative: Befriend the Beast

> “The shadow is 90% pure gold.” — Carl Jung

Jung’s idea wasn’t “let the monster run free.”  
It was: **get to know him.**  
Listen. Understand.  
Give the energy a *task*, a name, a shape.

> If not, he runs your life from the basement.

## 🛠️ So what can you *do* to begin?

This isn’t self-help fluff.  
This is alchemical, deep, soul work.  
But here’s a daily ritual to start integrating the shadow:

### 💀 Daily Shadow Ritual (5–10 minutes)

1. **End your day with a question:**  
   “Where did my shadow speak today?”  
   (Jealousy? Rage? Fantasies? Resentment? Lust? Criticism?)

2. **Don’t judge it. Don’t fix it. Just write it.**

3. Ask: *“What was it trying to protect me from?”*  
   Often, the answer is: *humiliation, invisibility, powerlessness.*

4. Thank it. Seriously.  
   “Thank you, Shadow. You were trying to help. Let’s do better next time — together.”

5. Breathe. Sleep.

Do this for a month.  
You will start to feel the shape of your darkness —  
and the gold it hides.
