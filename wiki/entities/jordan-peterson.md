# Jordan Peterson

## Overview

- Canadian clinical psychologist, professor (University of Toronto), and public intellectual.
- Known for writings and lectures on psychology, mythology, meaning, and political philosophy.
- Gained wide public prominence after 2016 for lectures and debates on free speech and identity politics.

## Key Ideas (as referenced in wiki sources)

- **[[concepts/writing-as-thinking]]**: Clear writing forces clear thinking; writing is not a record of thought but the mechanism of thought itself. A core pedagogical tool for precision and anti-ideological rigor.
- **Precision in language**: Defining loaded terms strips ideological language of its power.

## Major Works

- *Maps of Meaning* (1999)
- *12 Rules for Life: An Antidote to Chaos* (2018)
- *Beyond Order: 12 More Rules for Life* (2021)
