# Joseph Henrich

## Overview

- American anthropologist and evolutionary biologist; Professor at Harvard University (Department of Human Evolutionary Biology).
- Leading figure in the study of [[concepts/cultural-evolution]] and human behavioral diversity.

## Key Ideas (as referenced in wiki sources)

- **[[concepts/cultural-evolution]]**: Cultural practices and norms evolve through selection; those that persist tend to solve adaptive problems even when participants can't explain why.
- **WEIRD psychology**: Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, Democratic populations are psychological outliers — their traits resulted from specific cultural-evolutionary paths (e.g., medieval Church marriage rules breaking kinship networks).
- Certain psychological tools that reward nuance over certainty scaled human cooperation and innovation more effectively than dogmatic belief systems.
- Provides a non-partisan analytical frame: ask what selection pressures favored a norm rather than whether it is morally correct.

## Major Works

- *The WEIRDest People in the World* (2020)
