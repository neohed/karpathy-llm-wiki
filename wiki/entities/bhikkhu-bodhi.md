# Bhikkhu Bodhi

## Overview

American Theravada Buddhist monk and scholar (b. 1944). Known for rigorous, authoritative translations of Pali Canon texts and accessible scholarly works on Buddhist doctrine.

## Main Contributions

- *The Noble Eightfold Path: Way to the End of Suffering* — detailed scholarly exploration of the [[concepts/noble-eightfold-path]], including [[concepts/right-speech]] and [[concepts/right-intention]].
- Translations of major Pali texts (Majjhima Nikaya, Samyutta Nikaya).
- Founded the Buddhist Publication Society's educational series.
