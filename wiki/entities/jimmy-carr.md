# Jimmy Carr

## Overview

- British stand-up comedian, television presenter, and author.
- Known for dark, taboo-challenging humour and consistent prolific output (touring annually).
- Publicly articulate about his own philosophy of agency, gratitude, and craft.

## Key Ideas (as referenced in wiki sources)

- **Agency over victimhood**: placing personal responsibility at the centre of a productive life; rejecting entitlement.
- **Systems over goals**: daily habits and craft routines determine outcomes, not ambition or luck alone — echoes [[concepts/systems-vs-goals]].
- **Authenticity as competitive advantage**: "No one can beat you at being you."
- **Failure as pedagogy**: iterative test-fail-learn loop modelled on standup performance.
- **Comedy as social release valve**: shifting the Overton window; no sacred cows.
- **Freedom vs. authoritarianism**: rejects left-right political binary; frames politics around [[concepts/open-society]] values.
