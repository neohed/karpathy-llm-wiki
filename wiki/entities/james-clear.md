# James Clear

## Overview

American author and speaker, best known for *Atomic Habits* (2018), a guide to building good habits and breaking bad ones using behavioral science and practical frameworks.

## Main Contributions

- *Atomic Habits* (2018) — synthesizes psychology and behavioral science into a four-law practical framework for habit change.
- Popularized the idea of [[concepts/identity-based-habits]]: framing behavior change as identity change rather than outcome pursuit.
- Introduced the [[concepts/four-laws-of-behavior-change]] as a design toolkit.

- Recommends keeping identity flexible (defined by traits, not roles) to avoid rigidity that hinders growth (Ch. 20).
