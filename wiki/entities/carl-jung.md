# Carl Jung

## Overview

- Swiss psychiatrist and psychoanalyst (1875–1961); founder of analytical psychology.
- Student and later intellectual rival of Sigmund Freud.
- Introduced foundational concepts: the [[concepts/jungian-shadow]], archetypes, the collective unconscious, [[concepts/individuation]], the persona, anima/animus, and synchronicity.

## Key Ideas (as referenced in wiki sources)

- **[[concepts/jungian-shadow]]**: The repressed, disowned dimension of the psyche — not purely negative but containing "90% pure gold" of unlived potential.
- **[[concepts/shadow-integration]]**: The therapeutic and spiritual project of befriending rather than suppressing the shadow; giving its energy a task, name, and shape.
- **[[concepts/individuation]]**: The lifelong process of becoming a whole, integrated self by incorporating all parts of the psyche, including the shadow.

## Notable Quote

> "The shadow is 90% pure gold."
