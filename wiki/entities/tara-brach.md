# Tara Brach

## Overview

American psychologist and Buddhist meditation teacher. Known for integrating Western psychology with Buddhist practice, particularly around self-compassion and acceptance.

## Main Contributions

- *Radical Acceptance: Embracing Your Life with the Heart of a Buddha* — combines [[concepts/mindfulness]] and [[concepts/compassion-karuna]] (including self-compassion) with Western psychological frameworks.
- Teaches at the Insight Meditation Community of Washington, D.C.
- Popular podcast and talks on mindfulness, emotion, and self-compassion.
