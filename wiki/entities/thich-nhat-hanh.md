# Thich Nhat Hanh

## Overview

Vietnamese Zen Buddhist monk, peace activist, and prolific author (1926–2022). One of the most influential figures in bringing Buddhist mindfulness practice to Western audiences. Founded the Plum Village tradition.

## Main Contributions

- Coined the term **"Engaged Buddhism"** — applying Buddhist ethics to social and political issues.
- Authored over 100 books making Buddhist teachings accessible to lay practitioners worldwide.
- Key works referenced in [[sources/buddhist-principles]]:
  - *The Heart of the Buddha's Teaching* — comprehensive overview of core teachings including the [[concepts/noble-eightfold-path]].
  - *The Art of Communicating* — practical application of [[concepts/right-speech]].
  - *The Miracle of Mindfulness* — introductory guide to [[concepts/mindfulness]] practice.
