# Bhante Henepola Gunaratana

## Overview

Sri Lankan Theravada Buddhist monk (b. 1927), widely respected meditation teacher based in the United States. Best known for making vipassana (insight) meditation accessible to Western practitioners.

## Main Contributions

- *Mindfulness in Plain English* — classic, widely read introduction to [[concepts/mindfulness]] meditation and the cultivation of [[concepts/right-intention]] through practice. Praised for clear, practical guidance free of jargon.
- Founder of the Bhavana Society meditation center in West Virginia.
