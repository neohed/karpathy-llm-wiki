# Pema Chödrön

## Overview

American-born Tibetan Buddhist nun (b. 1936) and prolific author. One of the foremost Western teachers of Tibetan Buddhism, particularly known for practical guidance on facing difficulty and uncertainty with compassion and wisdom.

## Main Contributions

- *When Things Fall Apart: Heart Advice for Difficult Times* — applies [[concepts/mindfulness]], [[concepts/compassion-karuna]], and [[concepts/non-attachment]] to navigating personal crises, loss, and uncertainty.
- *The Places That Scare You*, *Start Where You Are*, and many other works on groundlessness and courage.
- Resident teacher at Gampo Abbey, Nova Scotia.
