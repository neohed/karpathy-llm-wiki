# Stoicism

## Overview

A Hellenistic philosophical school (founded ~300 BCE by Zeno of Citium) emphasising virtue, reason, and the dichotomy of control — distinguishing between what is within our power (judgements, intentions, responses) and what is not (external events, others' actions).

## Core Tenets

- **Dichotomy of control**: focus only on what you can influence; accept what you cannot.
- **Virtue as the only good**: external goods (wealth, fame, health) are "preferred indifferents" — desirable but not necessary for eudaimonia.
- **Negative visualisation (premeditatio malorum)**: imagining loss to build resilience and gratitude.
- **Discipline of desire**: align desires with what is actually achievable and within your control.
