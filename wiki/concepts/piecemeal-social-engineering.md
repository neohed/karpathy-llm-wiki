# Piecemeal Social Engineering

## Overview

[[entities/karl-popper]]'s term for the correct method of social reform: **modest, reversible, and testable** interventions that tackle specific ills rather than attempting wholesale societal redesign.

## Key Points

- Mirrors the scientific method: form a hypothesis (the reform), test it, observe results, revise or reverse.
- Opposed to utopian/holistic social engineering, which attempts to redesign the whole social order according to a blueprint.
- Failures of piecemeal reforms are visible and correctable; failures of utopian redesigns tend to be catastrophic and irreversible.
- Aligns with [[entities/edmund-burke]]'s prudent gradualism and [[entities/friedrich-hayek]]'s evolutionary caution.

## Software Engineering Analogy

Equivalent to: make small, tested, reversible code changes rather than wholesale rewrites without safety nets.
