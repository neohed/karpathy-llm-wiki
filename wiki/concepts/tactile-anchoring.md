# Tactile Anchoring

## Overview

A [[concepts/grounding]] technique using a physical object — chosen for its texture, weight, or personal meaning — as a **somatic anchor** to the present moment. Touching it with intentional attention interrupts dissociation, flashbacks, cravings, and anxiety spirals.

## How It Works

- The sensation of touch (texture, temperature, weight) activates the sensory cortex and pulls attention out of the threat-response loop.
- Pairing the touch with breath and an anchoring statement ("I'm here now") adds cognitive and respiratory regulation layers.
- The object becomes **conditioned** over time — its presence alone begins to signal safety, similar to how environmental cues condition habits in [[concepts/environment-design]].

## Object Suggestions (from [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]])

- Soft scarf, wooden bead, gym wrist wrap, tech gadget, gym weight.
- Something personally meaningful — the source notes a desire for a **"sacred object"**.

## Applications

- On desk while WFH — anytime isolation or flashbacks hit.
- Sobriety anchor — touch during cravings or avoidance urges.
- Micro-win reinforcement — touch after completing a task to mark progress.
- Content creation — touch before/after posting or creating.
