# Mindfulness (Sati)

## Overview

One of the central practices in Buddhism and a widely adopted secular tool for mental well-being. **Mindfulness (Pali: Sati)** is the practice of maintaining full, non-judgmental awareness of the present moment — including thoughts, emotions, and bodily sensations.

## Key Points

- Develops self-awareness and emotional regulation.
- Reduces stress and anxiety by anchoring attention in the present rather than in rumination or anticipation.
- Promotes mental clarity and balanced responses to life's challenges.
- Core component of the [[concepts/noble-eightfold-path]] (Right Mindfulness, Samma Sati).

## Forms of Practice

- Formal meditation (breath awareness, body scan, loving-kindness).
- Mindful breathing integrated into daily life.
- Mindful eating, walking, and other activities.
