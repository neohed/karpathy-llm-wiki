# Right Intention (Samma Sankappa)

## Overview

One of the eight components of the [[concepts/noble-eightfold-path]]. Right Intention addresses the motivations and mental orientations underlying action — cultivating thoughts of goodwill, compassion, and non-harm as the foundation for ethical living.

## Three Aspects

1. **Goodwill (Nekkhamma / Abyapada)** — intentions of kindness and benevolence; the antithesis of ill will.
2. **Compassion** — empathy for others' suffering; motivation to alleviate it through action.
3. **Non-Attachment / Non-Harm** — intentions free from craving, aggression, or desire to harm.

## Practical Applications

- **Mindful decision-making**: reflect on your intentions before acting; check alignment with values.
- **Setting positive goals**: prioritize well-being, compassion, and positive impact over ego or status.
- **Meditation and mindfulness**: develop awareness of habitual thought patterns and intentions.

## Benefits

- Greater inner peace and contentment through reduced inner conflict.
- More ethical and consistent decision-making.
- Supports personal growth and the cultivation of compassion.
