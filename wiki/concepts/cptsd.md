# CPTSD (Complex Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder)

## Overview

**Complex PTSD** arises from prolonged, repeated trauma (as opposed to single-incident PTSD) — commonly from abuse, assault, captivity, or prolonged neglect. It differs from PTSD in its pervasive effects on identity, emotional regulation, relationships, and self-perception.

## Key Symptoms Referenced in [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]]

- **Flashbacks** — intrusive re-experiencing of traumatic events.
- **Hypervigilance** — chronic threat-detection; the nervous system locked in fight/flight.
- **Isolation** — social withdrawal; difficulty with connection and trust.
- **Shame** — pervasive sense of defectiveness or worthlessness.
- **Avoidance** — binge behaviors, procrastination as escape from distress.
- **Fear of exposure** — anxiety about sharing oneself publicly (content creation, online posting).

## Somatic Approach to CPTSD

- Trauma is stored in the body; recovery requires body-based intervention, not only cognitive processing.
