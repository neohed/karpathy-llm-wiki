# Right Speech (Samma Vaca)

## Overview

One of the eight components of the [[concepts/noble-eightfold-path]]. Right Speech prescribes using language in a way that is truthful, kind, constructive, and timely — and refraining from speech that causes harm or discord.

## Four Aspects

1. **Truthfulness** — speak honestly; avoid deceit, lies, and misleading others.
2. **Kindness / Compassion** — use gentle, considerate words; avoid abusive or hurtful language.
3. **Constructive Communication** — speak helpfully and purposefully; avoid idle gossip and divisive speech.
4. **Timeliness** — consider the appropriate moment and context before speaking.

## Practical Applications

- **Active listening**: understand before responding.
- **Clear communication**: ensure messages are understood as intended.
- **Empathetic feedback**: focus on solutions, not criticism.
- **Avoiding gossip**: refrain from idle or harmful talk.

## Benefits

- Builds trust and integrity in relationships.
- Reduces conflict and misunderstanding.
- Creates more respectful, supportive environments (personal and professional).
