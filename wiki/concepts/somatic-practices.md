# Somatic Practices

## Overview

**Somatic practices** are therapeutic and self-care techniques that work through the body — sensation, movement, breath, and touch — rather than (or in addition to) cognitive processing. Rooted in the understanding that trauma is stored in the body and must be released through the body.

## Core Premise

- The nervous system, not just the mind, holds trauma.
- Cognitive insight alone is insufficient for trauma recovery; the body must be involved.
- Regulation of the autonomic nervous system (especially via the [[concepts/vagus-nerve]]) is the physiological target.

## Key Techniques (from [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]])

### Shaking / Tremoring
- Shake arms, legs, or whole body to release adrenaline and trauma tension.
- Pair with a low hum to engage the vagus nerve.
- Used before/after high-stakes activities (gym, online posting, content creation).

### Grounding
- Pre-task sitting/stillness with hand on chest, deep breath, anchoring statement.
- [[concepts/tactile-anchoring]]: a textured object touched with intention to anchor to the present.

### Body Scan
- Lie or sit, scan from toes to head, notice sensations without judgment.
- Morning reset tool; also a foundational [[concepts/mindfulness]] technique.

### Embodied Movement
- Weight training focused on sensation rather than performance metrics.
- Power poses with gentle sway during affirmations.
- Cool-down stretches (forward fold, child's pose) with attentional focus.

### Somatic Journaling
- Stream-of-consciousness writing about body sensations (e.g., "Chest heavy, feet grounded").
- Externalizes trauma somatically; reduces shame's grip.

### Embodied Affirmations
- Affirmations spoken while moving (swaying, power posing) to integrate them physically, not just cognitively.

## Applications

- **Trauma recovery** — releasing stored fight/flight activation.
- **Shame release** — shaking before/after shame-triggering activities.
- **Transition rituals** — shifting from passive (binge-viewing) to active (content creation) states.
- **Social anxiety** — somatic prep before online posting or calls.
- **Sobriety support** — tactile anchors during cravings.

## Connection to Other Concepts

- Overlaps significantly with [[concepts/mindfulness]] — both emphasize present-moment body awareness.
- The **pre-habit ritual** function parallels [[concepts/motivation-rituals]] from habit science.
