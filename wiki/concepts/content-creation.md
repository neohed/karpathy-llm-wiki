# Content Creation

## Overview

In [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]], **content creation** is the aspirational productive activity that the author is working toward — sharing AI/software engineering expertise online (X/Twitter, forums, video) — positioned as both a professional goal and a recovery milestone.

## Barriers Identified

- **Fear of exposure**: posting publicly feels threatening; trauma activates fight/flight around visibility.
- **Perfectionism / self-doubt**: "What value do I have?" — framed as trauma talking.
- **Binge-avoidance cycle**: passive consumption (binge-viewing) as escape from the vulnerability of creation.
- **Isolation**: lack of connection makes the creative act feel high-stakes.

## Strategies to Enable It

- **Somatic preparation**: shake, breathe, affirm before posting or creating.
- **Anonymous testing**: low-stakes X account to build tolerance for exposure.
- **Micro-task approach**: 5-minute timer, one tweet, one outline — mirrors [[concepts/two-minute-rule]].
- **Time boundary**: cap passive consumption at 10 PM; shift to creation.
- **Community engagement**: tech forums and r/MachineLearning for identity reinforcement via contribution.

## Thematic Content Areas (Implied)

- AI / LLMs (vLLM insights, AI tips)
- Software engineering (Grafana tricks, defense-sector context)
- 26 years of professional experience as the knowledge base

## Connection to Recovery

- Content creation is framed as an expression of **reclaimed voice** — parallel to [[concepts/shadow-integration]]'s "reclaiming fire" and the ability to "own one's work."
- Each post is a [[concepts/micro-wins]] event, not a performance metric.
