# Cognitive Dissonance Tolerance

## Overview

The capacity to **sit with conflicting data or opposing views** without prematurely resolving the tension via a default narrative. Proposed as a core teachable skill for sovereign, anti-ideological thinking.

## Key Points

- The brain demands certainty; premature narrative closure is a cognitive shortcut that feels satisfying but truncates understanding.
- Discomfort from holding two opposing views simultaneously is a **signal of deeper engagement**, not a problem to be eliminated.
- Practice: after reading two opposing sources, write a one-paragraph summary that steel-mans *both* without choosing a side.
- Analogised to engineering: the discomfort of working with a messy legacy system before fully understanding it — premature refactoring causes regressions.

## Relation to Other Concepts

- Prerequisite for effective [[concepts/steel-manning]].
- Enabled by [[concepts/epistemic-humility]] — if you're comfortable not knowing, you can hold competing hypotheses longer.
- Connects to [[concepts/systems-thinking]]: complex systems generate genuine trade-offs with no clean resolution.
