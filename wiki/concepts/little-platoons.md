# Little Platoons

## Overview

A phrase from [[entities/edmund-burke]] referring to **family, local community, and intermediate institutions** — the small-scale associations through which individuals develop loyalty, belonging, and civic virtue.

## Key Points

- These intermediate bodies protect individual liberty more effectively than central power, acting as buffers between the individual and the state.
- Dissolving them in favour of direct state–individual relationships weakens the social fabric and concentrates power dangerously.
- Anticipates later concepts of civil society and subsidiarity.
- Complements [[entities/karl-popper]]'s [[concepts/open-society]] (institutional checks) and [[entities/friedrich-hayek]]'s [[concepts/rule-of-law]] (limits on central power).
