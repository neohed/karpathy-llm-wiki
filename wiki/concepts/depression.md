# Depression

## Overview

A state of persistent low mood, loss of motivation, and diminished sense of meaning. One of the most widespread mental health conditions.

## In Jungian / Shadow-Work Frame

- [[sources/shadow-work]] frames depression as **"the soul's protest against living a life that isn't whole"** — the psychic cost of a persona-dominated existence in which authentic self-expression is chronically suppressed.
- One of three primary consequences of shadow suppression, alongside [[concepts/burnout]] and [[concepts/addiction]].
- [[concepts/shadow-integration]] is proposed as a pathway toward recovery by restoring wholeness and reclaiming repressed vitality.
