# Nervous System Regulation

## Overview

The process of shifting the autonomic nervous system from a **sympathetic** (fight/flight/freeze) state to a **parasympathetic** (rest/digest/connect) state. A central goal of trauma-informed [[concepts/somatic-practices]].

## Why It Matters in Trauma Recovery

- In [[concepts/cptsd]], the nervous system is dysregulated as a default: threat-detection is chronically activated even in safe environments.
- Cognitive insight cannot directly override this — body-based interventions are required.
- Regulation creates the physiological safety needed for connection, creativity, and healing.

## Key Regulatory Levers (from [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]])

- **Deep, slow breathing** — activates the parasympathetic system directly.
- **Humming / low vocalization** — stimulates the [[concepts/vagus-nerve]] via vibration.
- **Shaking / tremoring** — releases stored adrenaline and cortisol.
- **Slow stretching** — downregulates arousal via proprioceptive feedback.
- **Touch / warmth** — hand on chest, tactile objects.
- **Stillness with intention** — sitting before action (pre-gym grounding, pre-post ritual).
