# Trauma

## Overview

**Trauma** refers to the lasting psychological and physiological impact of overwhelming or threatening experiences that exceed a person's capacity to cope. It disrupts the nervous system, memory, identity, and relational functioning.

## Types Referenced in Sources

- **Single-incident trauma** (assault) — implied in [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]].
- **Complex / repeated trauma** — associated with [[concepts/cptsd]]; prolonged exposure.

## Body Storage

- Trauma is encoded in the body, not just the mind — muscle tension, hypervigilance, and somatic reactivity are physical residue.
- Recovery therefore requires [[concepts/somatic-practices]], not only cognitive processing.

## Manifestations Referenced

- Flashbacks, hypervigilance, shame, isolation, avoidance, addiction, depression, burnout.
- See also: [[concepts/cptsd]], [[concepts/shame]], [[concepts/burnout]], [[concepts/addiction]], [[concepts/depression]].

## Jungian Lens

- Unprocessed trauma feeds the [[concepts/jungian-shadow]] — repressed pain becomes shadow energy.
- [[concepts/shadow-integration]] and somatic release both address trauma's residue through different modalities.
