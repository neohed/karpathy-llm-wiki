# Vagus Nerve

## Overview

The tenth cranial nerve and the primary conduit of the **parasympathetic nervous system**. It runs from the brainstem through the neck, chest, and abdomen, innervating the heart, lungs, and gut. Central to [[concepts/nervous-system-regulation]] and trauma-informed somatic therapy.

## Role in Regulation

- Higher **vagal tone** correlates with greater emotional regulation, social engagement, and resilience.
- Activating the vagus nerve shifts the body from sympathetic (fight/flight) to parasympathetic (rest/connect) mode.

## Activation Techniques (from [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]])

- **Humming / low vocalization**: vibration in the throat stimulates vagal branches directly.
- **Deep, slow breathing**: elongated exhalation activates the vagal brake on heart rate.
- **Gentle movement / shaking**: proprioceptive input engages vagal pathways.
- **Warmth and touch**: hand on chest, skin contact.
