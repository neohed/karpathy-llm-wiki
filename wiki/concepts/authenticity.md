# Authenticity

## Overview

The quality of acting in accordance with one's genuine character, values, and strengths rather than imitating others or performing a constructed persona. Framed by [[entities/jimmy-carr]] as a competitive advantage: "No one can beat you at being you."

## Key Points

- Authenticity sidesteps zero-sum competition: you occupy a unique niche by being fully yourself.
- "Factory settings": accept and lean into innate traits rather than fighting them.
- Discovering authentic strengths requires experimentation — see [[concepts/explore-exploit-strategy]].
- Closely related to [[concepts/identity-based-habits]]: habits built on genuine identity are more durable than those imposed externally.

## Tension

- Authenticity does not mean stasis — [[entities/jimmy-carr]] also endorses reinvention (changing environment, rewriting narrative). True authenticity involves discovering who you are through action, not assuming it a priori.
