# Somatic Journaling

## Overview

A journaling practice focused on **body sensations rather than narrative or analysis**. Writing stream-of-consciousness about physical experience (e.g., "Chest heavy, feet grounded") rather than events or interpretations.

## From [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]]

- 5 minutes weekly.
- Write without editing or judgment.
- Let the hand move intuitively.
- Purpose: **externalizes trauma somatically**, reducing shame's grip.
- Weekly format: write about how body feels while creating content or maintaining sobriety.

## How It Differs from Standard Journaling

- Standard journaling: narrative, cognitive, analytical.
- Somatic journaling: sensation-based, pre-cognitive, body-first.
- Closer to [[concepts/writing-as-thinking]] in its unedited quality, but directed inward at the body rather than outward at ideas.

## Connection to Other Practices
