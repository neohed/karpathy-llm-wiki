# Open Society

## Overview

[[entities/karl-popper]]'s positive political ideal: a society that values **individual freedom, institutional checks, and the peaceful correction of errors** through criticism and democratic processes.

## Key Points

- Defined in opposition to "closed societies" that suppress criticism in the name of a final Truth or historical destiny.
- Requires freedom of expression and robust institutions for peaceful political change.
- Relies on [[concepts/falsification]] logic: errors should be identifiable and correctable without violence or revolution.
- Complementary to [[entities/friedrich-hayek]]'s [[concepts/rule-of-law]] and [[entities/edmund-burke]]'s intermediate institutions ([[concepts/little-platoons]]).
