# Rule of Law

## Overview

The principle that governance should operate through **general, abstract, and publicly known rules** rather than through the discretionary will of officials or the engineering of specific outcomes.

## Key Points

- Central to [[entities/friedrich-hayek]]'s political theory: general rules allow individuals to plan their own lives; discretionary state power does not.
- Prevents arbitrary coercion and concentrations of power.
- Complements [[entities/edmund-burke]]'s emphasis on prescription and long-established legal forms.
- Part of the broader [[concepts/ordered-liberty]] framework of the [[concepts/burke-hayek-popper-triad]].
