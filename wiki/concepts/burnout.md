# Burnout

## Overview

A state of chronic exhaustion — physical, emotional, and cognitive — resulting from prolonged stress, overextension, or misalignment between a person's values and their lived reality.

## In Jungian / Shadow-Work Frame

- [[sources/shadow-work]] frames burnout as **repressed rage + self-betrayal**: the energy cost of continuously suppressing authentic responses and performing a persona.
- Burnout is one of three primary consequences of shadow suppression, alongside [[concepts/addiction]] and [[concepts/depression]].
- [[concepts/shadow-integration]] is proposed as the path to relief — reducing the inner warfare of self-monitoring releases energy.
