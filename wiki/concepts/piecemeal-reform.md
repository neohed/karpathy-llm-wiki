# Piecemeal Reform

## Overview

The shared commitment of the [[concepts/burke-hayek-popper-triad]] to **incremental, experimental, and reversible change** rather than radical restructuring.

## Key Points

- [[entities/edmund-burke]]: Prudent gradualism; change should be rooted in existing forms and slow.
- [[entities/friedrich-hayek]]: Evolutionary caution; respect what has survived the test of time.
- [[entities/karl-popper]]: Explicit methodological commitment via [[concepts/piecemeal-social-engineering]].
- Radical leaps are dangerous because they destroy embedded information in [[concepts/evolved-institutions]] and make errors difficult to diagnose or reverse.
