# Repression

## Overview

A psychological defense mechanism in which unwanted thoughts, feelings, impulses, or memories are pushed out of conscious awareness into the unconscious. Central to both Freudian psychoanalysis and Jungian depth psychology.

## In Jungian Context

- Repression is the primary mechanism by which the **[[concepts/jungian-shadow]]** is formed and maintained.
- Repressed content does not disappear — it accumulates in the shadow and exerts autonomous influence on behavior, emotion, and perception.
- In [[sources/shadow-work]]: *"Suppressing the shadow doesn't destroy it. It burrows."*
- Long-term repression manifests as [[concepts/burnout]], [[concepts/addiction]], and [[concepts/depression]].
