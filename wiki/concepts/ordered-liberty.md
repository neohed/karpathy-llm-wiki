# Ordered Liberty

## Overview

The positive political ideal of the [[concepts/burke-hayek-popper-triad]]: **freedom sustained and protected by evolved institutions, law, and social norms** — as opposed to either anarchic licence or state-engineered equality.

## Key Points

- The triad is explicitly *not* reactionary nostalgia; it is a positive programme.
- Liberty requires an ordered framework: [[concepts/rule-of-law]], [[concepts/little-platoons]], open criticism ([[concepts/open-society]]), and [[concepts/evolved-institutions]].
- The goal is a system that is robust to human failure — one that prevents catastrophic outcomes even when individuals or governments act badly.
- Distinguishes classical liberalism and conservative thought from both libertarian atomism and progressive statism.
