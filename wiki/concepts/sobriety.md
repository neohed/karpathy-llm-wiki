# Sobriety

## Overview

In the context of [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]], sobriety refers to the active recovery from addictive or avoidant behaviors — binge-viewing, substance use (implied), and compulsive escape patterns. It is framed as a **concurrent recovery goal** alongside trauma healing, not as separate from it.

## Somatic Sobriety Practices

- **Somatic sobriety anchor**: a tactile object on desk; touch during cravings, saying "Sobriety gives me clarity." Follow with a purposeful task to redirect energy.
- **Time boundaries**: set a viewing cutoff (10 PM); stand, shake, affirm, shift to creation.
- **Morning reset**: body scan + one goal reclaims the day from avoidance patterning.

## Connection to Trauma

- Binge behaviors and substance use are framed in [[sources/shadow-work]] as *"pain that couldn't be voiced"* — addiction as repressed shadow.
- Somatic practices address the same dysregulation that drives addictive behavior.
- Sobriety is not willpower alone but nervous system regulation + environmental design.

## Habit Science Parallel

- The somatic sobriety anchor mirrors [[concepts/commitment-devices]] and [[concepts/environment-design]] from [[sources/atomic-habits-part-03]] — restructuring context to interrupt the craving → response cycle.
