# Flow State

## Overview

A state of deep, engaged focus and intrinsic satisfaction that occurs when a task is at the right difficulty level — challenging enough to demand attention, achievable enough to sustain it. Associated with [[entities/mihaly-csikszentmihalyi]] (originator of flow theory); referenced in [[entities/james-clear]]'s *Atomic Habits* (Chapter 19).

## Conditions

- Task difficulty is approximately **4% beyond current skill level** (per *Atomic Habits*).
- Neither too easy (boredom) nor too hard (anxiety).
- Clear goals and immediate feedback support entry into flow.

## Relation to the Goldilocks Rule

- The [[concepts/goldilocks-rule]] is the habit-design principle for sustaining motivation at the edge of ability — flow is the psychological state that results when that balance is achieved.
