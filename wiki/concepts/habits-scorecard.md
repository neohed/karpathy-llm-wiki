# Habits Scorecard

## Overview

A self-audit tool introduced in [[entities/james-clear]]'s *Atomic Habits* (Chapter 4) for surfacing unconscious habits. Part of the **1st Law: Make It Obvious**.

## How It Works

1. List all your daily behaviors in sequence from morning to night.
2. Label each: positive (+), negative (–), or neutral (=).
3. Observe without judgment. The goal is awareness, not immediate change.

## Purpose

- Makes the invisible visible: habitual behaviors run on autopilot; the scorecard interrupts that automaticity.
- Builds the mindfulness prerequisite for intentional habit design.
- Key question for each behavior: *"Does this help me become the type of person I wish to be?"*
