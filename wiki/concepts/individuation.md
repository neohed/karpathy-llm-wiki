# Individuation

## Overview

The central developmental concept in [[entities/carl-jung]]'s analytical psychology: the **lifelong process of becoming a whole, integrated self** by consciously incorporating all parts of the psyche — including the [[concepts/jungian-shadow]], the anima/animus, the persona, and archetypal patterns — rather than identifying exclusively with any one part.

## Key Points

- Individuation is not self-improvement in a superficial sense; it is a deep psychological and spiritual maturation.
- It requires encountering and integrating disowned parts of the self — especially the shadow.
- The result is not perfection but **wholeness**: a self that can hold contradictions without being destroyed by them.
- Related to [[concepts/shadow-integration]] as its primary practical pathway.
