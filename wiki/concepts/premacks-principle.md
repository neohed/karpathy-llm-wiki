# Premack's Principle

## Overview

A principle from behavioral psychology stating that **more probable (preferred) behaviors can reinforce less probable (less preferred) behaviors**. Named after psychologist David Premack.

## Application in Atomic Habits

Cited by [[entities/james-clear]] (Chapter 8) as the theoretical basis for [[concepts/temptation-bundling]]: linking a wanted behavior (high probability) to a needed behavior (lower probability) increases the likelihood of doing the needed behavior.

## Classic Examples

- "You can watch TV after you finish your homework."
- "You can have dessert after eating your vegetables."

## Key Point

- The more desirable activity acts as a reward that makes the less desirable activity attractive by association.
