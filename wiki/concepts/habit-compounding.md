# Habit Compounding

## Overview

The principle, central to [[entities/james-clear]]'s *Atomic Habits*, that **small, consistent behaviors accumulate exponentially over time** — analogous to compound interest. Tiny improvements (or declines) that appear negligible in the short term produce dramatic long-term divergence.

## Key Points

- 1% improvement daily ≈ 37× better over a year; 1% decline daily ≈ near zero.
- The compounding effect is invisible in early stages — see [[concepts/plateau-of-latent-potential]].
- Both positive habits (learning, health, relationships) and negative habits (stress, outrage, avoidance) compound.
- Time is the multiplier: what you repeat becomes who you are.

## Implications

- Don't evaluate habits by their immediate impact; evaluate by their long-term trajectory.
- Small daily choices carry disproportionate long-term weight.
