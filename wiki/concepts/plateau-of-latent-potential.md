# Plateau of Latent Potential

## Overview

Term from [[entities/james-clear]]'s *Atomic Habits* describing the **gap between effort invested and visible results** in the early stages of habit formation. Change accumulates beneath the surface until a critical threshold is crossed, at which point results appear suddenly.

## Key Points

- Analogy: heating an ice cube from 25°F to 31°F produces no visible change; at 32°F it melts. The earlier heating was not wasted — it was necessary.
- Most people abandon habits during the plateau, mistaking invisible accumulation for failure.
- Breakthrough results feel sudden but are the product of sustained prior effort.
- Patience and consistency are the primary virtues required to cross the threshold.

## Practical Implication

- Expect a lag between action and visible result. Judge a system by its trajectory, not its current position.
