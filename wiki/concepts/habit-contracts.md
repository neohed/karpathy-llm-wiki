# Habit Contracts

## Overview

A written or verbal agreement in which you commit to a specific behavior and define clear, enforceable penalties for failure. From [[entities/james-clear]]'s *Atomic Habits* (Chapter 17). A formal implementation of the **inversion of the 4th Law: Make It Unsatisfying**.

## Structure

- State the specific behavior and frequency.
- Define concrete, meaningful penalties for non-compliance.
- Have one or more [[concepts/accountability-partner]]s sign the contract to add social expectation and reputational risk.

## Why It Works

- Creates an immediate and emotional cost to failure.
- Consequences should be **specific, meaningful, and consistently applied** — vague penalties are easy to rationalize away.
- Parallels how societal contracts (laws, regulations) shape public behavior through defined consequences.

## Examples

- Agreeing to pay a friend $50 for every missed workout.
- Publicly committing to donate to a disliked cause if a goal is not met.
- Daily check-in texts with a specific question: "Did you do X today?"
