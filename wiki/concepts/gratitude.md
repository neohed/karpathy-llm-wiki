# Gratitude

## Overview

The disposition to recognise and appreciate what one has — including talents, opportunities, and relationships — as a foundation for happiness and resilience. Described by [[entities/jimmy-carr]] as the "mother of all virtues."

## Key Points

- Gratitude reframes luck (talent, circumstance, opportunity) as gift rather than injustice.
- Acts as an antidote to entitlement and bitterness.
- Empirically linked to wellbeing in positive psychology literature.
- Functions as a cognitive shift: from gap-awareness (what is missing) to asset-awareness (what is present) — inverts the [[concepts/dopamine-driven-feedback-loop]] pattern of perpetual wanting.

## Relation to Buddhist Concepts

- Overlaps with [[concepts/non-attachment]]: both reduce suffering caused by clinging to desired states.
- Related to [[concepts/mindfulness]]: present-moment awareness is a precondition for noticing what one already has.
