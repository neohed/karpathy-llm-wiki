# Embodied Affirmation

## Overview

Affirmations spoken or whispered **while moving or holding a physical posture**, integrating positive self-talk with somatic experience rather than delivering it purely cognitively. The premise: words alone may not penetrate trauma-conditioned self-rejection; pairing them with body sensation anchors them more deeply.

## Techniques (from [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]])

- **Power pose + sway**: stand hands on hips (like gym stance), sway gently, repeat "I survived, and my voice matters."
- **Hand-on-heart**: place hand on chest, feel warmth, say "My knowledge is valuable. I don't need to be perfect."
- **Post-task stretch**: sway/stretch while saying "I took a step, and that's enough."
- **Somatic shame release**: shake first, then rest hand on chest, say "This is trauma talking, not truth."

## Why Embodiment Matters

- In [[concepts/cptsd]], the thinking mind often cannot override the body's fear response.
- Movement + breath + words engages multiple regulatory systems simultaneously.
- The physical state (warmth, movement, stable posture) conditions the words to feel true rather than hollow.

## Connection to Other Concepts

- Parallels [[concepts/motivation-rituals]]: the physical act creates a conditioned association.
- The power pose element overlaps with [[concepts/power-pose]] — using body language to influence internal state.
