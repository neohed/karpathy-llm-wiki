# Long-Term Potentiation

## Overview

A neurological mechanism by which **repeated activation of neural pathways strengthens their connections**, making future activation faster and more automatic. Summarized by the phrase: *"Neurons that fire together wire together."*

## Application in Atomic Habits

Cited by [[entities/james-clear]] (Chapter 11) as the biological basis for habit formation through repetition. Each time a behavior is performed, the underlying neural pathway is reinforced:
- Early repetitions: high cognitive effort, slow.
- With repetition: effort decreases, speed increases.
- Eventually: the behavior crosses the **habit line** into automaticity.

## Key Implication

- **Frequency matters more than duration**: forming a habit depends on how often the behavior is repeated, not how long you've been attempting it.
- Focus should be on increasing the number of successful repetitions, not chasing a magic number of days.
