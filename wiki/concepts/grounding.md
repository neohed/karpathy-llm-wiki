# Grounding

## Overview

Techniques that anchor awareness to the present moment — particularly to the physical body and immediate environment — interrupting dissociation, flashbacks, anxiety spirals, and hypervigilance. A core tool in trauma recovery and [[concepts/cptsd]] management.

## Mechanism

- Activates the parasympathetic nervous system ("rest and digest") to counteract fight/flight activation.
- Re-establishes a sense of "I am here, now, and I am safe."
- Works through physical sensation, breath, and intentional attention.

## Techniques (from [[sources/therapy-grok-survivor-tips]])

- **Hand on chest + deep breath**: simple pre-task ritual; the warmth and pressure signal safety.
- **Tactile anchoring**: touching a textured object (scarf, wooden bead, wrist wrap) while focusing on its feel.
- **Shaking / tremoring**: releases hypervigilant tension, resets the nervous system.
- **Body scan**: slow scan from toes to head, noticing sensation without judgment.
- **Environmental anchoring**: noticing the room, body weight in chair, feet on floor.

## Affirmations as Grounding Reinforcement

- "I'm safe here."
- "I'm here now."
- "This is for my body's healing."
- Spoken quietly or whispered; the act of speaking engages the [[concepts/vagus-nerve]].
