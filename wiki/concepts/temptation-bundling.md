# Temptation Bundling

## Overview

A habit-design strategy from [[entities/james-clear]]'s *Atomic Habits* (Chapter 8) for making habits more attractive by **linking a behavior you *need* to do with one you *want* to do**.

## Basis

Grounded in [[concepts/premacks-principle]]: more desirable behaviors can reinforce less desirable ones.

## Formula

> *After [CURRENT HABIT], I will [HABIT I NEED].*
> *After [HABIT I NEED], I will [HABIT I WANT].*

## Example

- *After I pour my morning coffee, I will journal one gratitude item. After journaling, I will read the news.*
- *After I sit down on the exercise bike, I will listen to my favorite podcast. After my workout, I will have my smoothie.*

## Key Points

- Applies the **2nd Law of Behavior Change**: Make It Attractive.
- Works by borrowing the motivational pull of a desired activity and attaching it to a less appealing habit.
- Can be combined with [[concepts/habit-stacking]] to embed the bundle inside an existing routine.
- Not every habit can become a supernormal stimulus, but every habit can be made *more* attractive through strategic pairing.
