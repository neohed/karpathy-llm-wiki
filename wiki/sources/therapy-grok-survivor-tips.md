# Therapy - Grok, Survivor Tips

## Abstract / Overview

A personal note compiling somatic therapy practices — likely generated with AI assistance (Grok) — oriented toward trauma recovery, specifically for a survivor navigating isolation, sobriety, CPTSD symptoms, and a desire to return to content creation. Practices are organized by context: gym, home, content creation, social re-engagement, and time management. Includes a structured weekly implementation plan.

## Key Themes

### Somatic Grounding
- The central modality throughout is **somatic practice**: using body sensation, movement, and breath to regulate the nervous system rather than cognitive reframing alone.
- The nervous system (specifically the [[concepts/vagus-nerve]]) is explicitly targeted via humming, shaking, deep breathing, and slow stretching.
- Repeated use of **tactile anchors** (textured objects, gym wrist wraps, tech gadgets) to interrupt flashbacks, cravings, and dissociation.

### Gym as Therapeutic Space
- Pre-gym grounding: sit in car 1–2 mins, hand on chest, repeat "This is for my body's healing."
- **Embodied weight training**: focus on sensations during lifts rather than reps — "somatic immersion."
- Post-workout shaking (2 minutes): shake limbs to release adrenaline and trauma tension; pair with low hum.
- Somatic cool-down: slow stretch (forward fold, child's pose) with whispered affirmation "I'm safe here."

### Home / WFH Micro-Practices
- **Morning body check-in** (2 mins): body scan, gentle sway/rock to release tension.
- **Tactile anchor** (anytime): textured object on desk, touch + breath when isolation or flashbacks hit.
- **Somatic journaling** (5 mins weekly): stream-of-consciousness body-feelings log, no editing.
- **Virtual connection prep**: shake hands / roll shoulders 30 seconds before online engagement.

### Somatic Transition: Binge-Viewing → Content Creation
- Pre-work ritual: shake 30–60 seconds, breath, affirmation, tactile anchor.
- Micro-task start: 5-minute timer for one tiny task; focus on physical act (typing, pen moving).
- Post-task release: sway/stretch 1 min, affirm "I took a step, and that's enough."
- Cap binge-viewing at set time (e.g., 10 PM); transition to 10 minutes of content work.

### Somatic Self-Compassion
- **Hand-on-heart anchor**: before posting, place hand on heart, feel warmth, affirm expertise.
- **Embodied affirmation**: power pose + gentle sway + "I survived, and my voice matters."
- **Somatic shame release**: if self-doubt spikes, shake 30 seconds, rest hand on chest, say "This is trauma talking, not truth."
- **Celebrate micro-wins**: touch a meaningful object and say "I showed up."
- Note: *"Need a sacred object"* — an intention flagged in the source.

### Micro-Connections / Social Re-Engagement
- Somatic prep before posting on X: shake hands/roll shoulders, affirmation, low-stakes first post (e.g., tech tip).
- Anonymous X account to share one AI/software engineering idea weekly.
- Weekly comment on tech forums (Stack Overflow, r/MachineLearning) using somatic prep.
- Optional: read one post on r/CPTSD weekly, shaking beforehand.

### Somatic Sobriety and Time Management
- **Somatic time boundary**: 10 PM viewing cutoff — stand, shake, say "I choose my future," shift to 10 min content work.
- **Somatic sobriety anchor**: tactile object on desk; touch when cravings or avoidance hit.
- **Morning reset**: body scan + one content goal to reclaim the day.
- **Content creation sprint**: 15-minute daily sprint (e.g., 1-min AI tip video, course module outline); shake before and after.

## Implementation Plan (Weekly Structure)

**Daily (5–10 min):**
- Morning: 2-min body scan + one content goal.
- Evening: 10 PM cutoff, 1-min shaking ritual, 5–10 min content work.
- Anytime: hand-on-heart if shame/self-doubt spikes.

**Gym Days (2–3×/week):**
- Pre-gym grounding, embodied lifting, post-gym 2-min idea jot, 1-min stretch cool-down.

**Weekly:**
- Post one anonymous X tweet (AI tip) after somatic prep.
- Comment on one tech forum thread.
- Write one somatic journal entry.
- Use somatic sobriety anchor daily, especially during cravings or late-night urges.
